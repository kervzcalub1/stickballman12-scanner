# How to Use the Scanner — PH Team

A simple guide for everyday use. No tech knowledge needed.

**What you do:** take each shoe the warehouse has scanned in, **put it online and
set its price**. You list it in **Intelligent Inventory (II)**, which then pushes
it out to the stores — **Alias, StockX, and Shopify** — and you tick off each
store as it's done.

---

## Signing in

1. Open the app link in your browser.
2. First time: tap **Create account**, fill in your details, choose **PH Team**,
   and submit. An admin has to approve you before you can log in.
3. After signing in you'll see three buttons:
   - **New Inventory** — newly received shoes to list
   - **Rescale Stock** — shoes that came back or are being re-listed
   - **No Box / Not Ready** — shoes not ready yet (view only)

---

## 1. Listing new shoes ("New Inventory")

This is your main screen — a monthly list of every shoe to put online.

1. Pick the **Month** and **Year** at the top (it opens on the current month).
   You can sort by date (oldest or newest).
2. Each line is **one shoe in one size**, with a **quantity** (for example
   "size 9 ×6" means six pairs). You list it **once** for all of them — no need to
   handle each pair separately.
3. To work a line, tap **Edit**, then fill in:
   - **Price**
   - **Intelligent Inventory** → **Yes** once you've added it to II
   - **Alias / StockX / Shopify** → **Yes** for each store it's now on
   - **Note** (optional)
4. Tap **Submit**. The line is saved and stamped with your name and time.

**The order to follow:** add the shoe to **Intelligent Inventory first** — that's
what sends it to the stores — then mark each store **Yes** as it goes live.

---

## 2. Working at the same time as teammates

Several PH people can be logged in at once, so the system keeps you from clashing:

- When you tap **Edit** on a line, everyone else sees a **🔒 lock with your name**
  on that line, and their **Edit button is disabled** for it. Only you can edit it.
- When you tap **Submit** (or **Cancel**), the line unlocks for others.
- If you open **Edit** and walk away without saving, the line **unlocks by itself
  after 1 hour** so it doesn't stay stuck.
- If two of you somehow try to save the same line, the system will tell the second
  person to **reload** so no one's work gets overwritten.

Locks appear and clear within a couple of seconds, so just watch for the 🔒.

---

## 3. Re-listed and returned shoes ("Rescale Stock")

Same kind of list and same steps as New Inventory, but for shoes the warehouse
**re-scanned** — returns, re-listings, recounts, transfers. Re-list these and set
their store/price the same way.

> Tip: a returned shoe may already show some stores as **Yes** from when it was
> first listed. Update the price and stores to match its current state.

---

## 4. Shoes that aren't ready ("No Box / Not Ready")

- Shoes received **without a box** are **not shown** in your New Inventory or
  Rescale lists, because they can't be sold yet.
- The **No Box / Not Ready** button lets you **see** what's pending. It's
  **view-only** for you — the warehouse fixes these. Once they do, the shoe
  appears in your normal list automatically.

---

## 5. When a shoe sells

You **don't** need to remove sold shoes from the stores. When the warehouse marks
a shoe **Sold**, the system **automatically takes it off Intelligent Inventory and
all the stores**. You'll see it noted in the shoe's history as system-generated.

---

## Helpful reminders

- **One line = one shoe + size + quantity.** Listing the line covers every pair in
  it.
- **Always add to Intelligent Inventory first**, then tick the stores.
- Watch for the **🔒** — if a teammate is editing a line, wait for it to clear.
- If you press **Back** or **refresh** while editing, the app warns you so you
  don't lose unsaved changes.
- Your name and time are saved on every change, so the team can always see who did
  what.

---

## If something goes wrong

- **The line won't let me edit (shows a lock):** a teammate is editing it — wait a
  moment for the lock to clear.
- **It said "just updated by someone else, reload":** another person saved that
  line first. Reload and re-do your change on the fresh numbers.
- **A shoe I expected isn't in the list:** check the **Month/Year** at the top. If
  it's a no-box shoe, it won't show until the warehouse fixes it.
- **My edit disappeared after I stepped away:** edits unlock after 1 hour of no
  activity — just tap **Edit** again and continue.
- **Still stuck:** tell your admin which shoe (and month) you were working on.

Shipments that were sold before they landed

Some shipments are already spoken for before the boxes arrive. Those pairs must not be listed — offering one would sell a pair that belongs to somebody else’s order. But the extra pairs on the same shipment are ordinary stock and do need listing. You are the one who says which is which.

Why this is your job and not the PH team’s You are holding the shipment, so you are the one who can tell which pairs an order covers. The PH team cannot open this page at all. Their part starts after you press Send for rescale.
1Tick Pre-sell when you start the shipment

On the Receiving screen, under the tracking number, tick Pre-sell shipment before you start scanning.

Receiving → Shipment step. One tick, for the whole shipment.
It is per shipment, not per pair The tick covers everything in the batch, including every box you add later. If a shipment is only partly pre-sold, still tick it — you tell the system which pairs are sold in step 4, and the rest are released.
If you forget to tick it The pairs go straight into normal stock and the PH team will list them — including the ones somebody has already bought. Tell an admin the batch number straight away.
2Receive it exactly as you always do

Nothing about intake changes. Same scanning, same 1ID stickers, same boxes, same review, same issues. Shelve the pairs as normal — they are real stock sitting on your shelves and they still need a location.

What is different, and it is invisible to you The pairs are held back from the PH team. They do not appear on the PH New Inventory screen and are not counted in any of the PH listing badges, so nobody can price or list them by accident.
3Open Pre-sell from the home screen

The card sits with Receiving Shipment Orders. The badge counts the pairs still waiting for an answer.

Home → Receiving Shipment Orders.

When any are waiting it also appears at the top of the screen under Needs attention.

Nothing on the shipment can be listed until this reaches zero.
4Say how many of each size are sold

Each shipment is listed shoe by shoe, size by size. For every size, type how many of them an order covers. Remains updates as you go — that is what will be released for listing.

19 arrived, 10 spoken for, 9 to list. Sizes with nothing left go grey.
If you want to…Do this
Count a rowType the number in the Sold box and press Tab or Enter. Best when a whole size run is spoken for.
Name one exact pairScan its 1ID into the box at the top of the page. Use this when a specific pair is the one that sold.
UndoType a smaller number. The extra pairs are handed straight back and become ordinary stock again.
Which pair does the system pick? When you type a count, it takes the oldest pairs of that size first. That is deliberate: the pairs are identical, unshelved and interchangeable, so nobody needs to decide which VIN carries which order.
A pre-sale that falls through Lower the number. The pairs come back as ordinary stock and go out for listing with the rest. Nothing is stuck.
5Send the rest for rescale

When the counts are right, press Send the N remaining for rescale. Those pairs go onto the PH team’s Rescale Stock worklist, where they are priced and listed to II and the stores.

The sold pairs stay put Anything you marked sold is left alone and stays on this page. It is spoken for, and listing it would offer somebody else’s pair for sale.
You can release more than once If a few more pairs turn out to be sold later, work the same shipment again: the ones you did not release are still here.

Marked sold is not the same as sold

The pair has not left the building yet.

StateWhat it means
Pre-soldAn order covers this pair. It is still on your shelf and has not shipped. It is not listed anywhere.
Sold / ShippedThe normal end of a pair’s life — reached the usual way, by scanning it out on Mark Sold / Mark Shipped when it actually leaves.
Do not mark them sold on the Mark Sold screen instead That is the final step for a pair that is going out the door today. If you use it early and the order collapses, the pair is stranded. Use the Pre-sell page — it can be undone.

Spotting a pre-sell shipment

The chip shows anywhere the shipment does.

Batches list. The same chip is on the batch page, on every box inside it, on Receiving → Recent, and on Inventory rows.

Packing an order:
scan, close, print, ship

Everything you send us goes out under a shipping label we have already paid for. This is how you record what is in each box, print the sheet that travels with it, and hand it to the courier.

Before you start You need your supplier sign-in, the boxes you are packing, and the shoes in front of you. Do this one box at a time — a label is closed and printed as a unit, so finishing one box before opening the next is what keeps the paperwork matching the contents.
1Sign in and open the order
  1. Go to the portal address you were given. It begins with supplier.
  2. Sign in. You land on your home screen with two cards — tap Purchase Orders.
  3. Your orders are listed by code (PO-101693). Open the one you are packing.
If you cannot sign in A supplier account only works on the supplier. address — signing in on our main site is refused on purpose. New accounts stay pending until we approve them. Forgotten password: use Forgot password?, then tell us, and we issue a temporary one.
Your shipments. Each row shows the order code, how many labels have shipped, and the total pairs declared so far.
2Find the label you are packing

Inside the order, each shipping label is one box on screen, shown with its courier tracking number. Match the number on screen to the number on the physical label you are about to stick on that box — that pairing is the whole point, and it is what tells us which parcel to expect the contents in.

The Ship to panel is where the boxes go. It is also printed on every manifest.

An order with two labels. Label 1 has three lines scanned into it; Label 2 is still empty.
3Scan the shoes into that label

Tap Add items on the label you are filling.

What this does and does not do Scanning here records what you intend to ship. It does not create stock on our side — we scan the box in ourselves when it lands, and compare our count to your list. That comparison is the reason the list needs to be right.
  1. Scan the barcode on the shoe box, or type the SKU and tap Add. The camera button scans with your phone.
  2. The shoe comes back with its size chips. Tap the size you are putting in the box.
  3. Tap the same size again to add another pair of it. Or use the + and − steppers.
  4. A size not in the list? Tap + Custom and type it.
  5. Enter Cost ea and Tip ea if you are declaring what you paid.
  6. Tap Add N units to label to commit the shoe.
  7. Keep going until the box is packed. Every line stays editable while the label is open — change a size, step the quantity, correct a price, or tap × to remove it.
Two sizes of one shoe, ready to add. The running total at the bottom is cost plus tips.
Cost and tip are per size, and optional The same shoe can cost you a different amount in a 9 than in an 11, so the money sits on the size's own row. Leaving them blank records nothing — it is not the same as entering $0. A new size row copies whatever you typed on the last one, so type the price once and only change the size that differs.
Scanning a different shoe If you scan a second shoe before adding the first, the app asks whether to Add & switch. That commits what is on screen and opens the new shoe — nothing is lost. Repeat scans of the same barcode within a second are ignored, so a scan gun firing twice will not double your count.
4Review and close the box

When the box is physically full, tap Review & close box and check the list against what is actually in front of you. This is the last easy moment to fix a miscount.

Keep editing takes you back to the lines.

Close box for shipment marks the label Ready to ship — packed and ready, but not yet gone.

  • A label needs at least one item before it can close.
  • Closing is reversible: Reopen to edit puts it back.
  • Closing does not tell the courier anything. That is step 6.
5Print the manifest, attach it, seal the box

The moment you close the box, the app puts these three steps in front of you. Do them now, while the box is still open in front of you.

  1. Tap Print manifest. The sheet downloads as a PDF — open the downloaded file and print it from there, on plain Letter paper.
  2. Attach the printed sheet to the box.
  3. Seal the box.
Reopening a label makes a printed sheet wrong The sheet is only guaranteed to match the contents at the moment you closed the box. If you reopen the label and change anything, throw the old sheet away and print it again — the Print manifest button stays on the label for exactly that reason.
Two buttons — use the right one Print manifest is the sheet that goes on the box, and it carries no prices. With my prices is the same list with your cost and tip on it, for checking your own numbers. Do not attach the priced one to the box.

What the printed manifest looks like

One sheet per box. This is what we open the parcel and check against.

Check these four things before you tape it on:

  • Box N of M — says which box of the shipment this is. If it reads "Box 1 of 2", we are expecting a second parcel.
  • Tracking number — must match the label physically stuck to this box.
  • Ship to — where the parcel is going.
  • Total pairs — count the shoes in the box against this number one last time.
Why it matters When the parcel reaches us, the warehouse counts what comes out of it and lines that up against this sheet. If the two disagree, this page is the evidence for what you say you sent.
6Ship the label

Mark the label shipped once the courier physically has the box — not when you close it, and not when you print the sheet.

A closed label. Reopen to edit, reprint the sheet, or ship it.
Shipping is the point of no return You are asked to confirm, and after that the items on that label can no longer be edited. If something is wrong, fix it before you ship.

Repeat steps 2–6 for each label. The whole order only flips to on its way to us once every label has been shipped.

If something goes wrong

The five questions we actually get asked.

SituationWhat to do
I scanned the wrong size or quantity Fix it on the line: retype the size, step the quantity, or tap × to remove it. Works any time the label is still open. If it is already closed, Reopen to edit first — then reprint the sheet.
The barcode will not scan Type the SKU into the same field instead and tap Add. If the shoe still does not come back, tap a size chip and enter it by hand — a typed line counts exactly the same as a scanned one.
I printed the sheet, then changed the box Throw the printed sheet away and tap Print manifest again. An out-of-date sheet is worse than none — it is what we count against.
I shipped a label by mistake You cannot undo it yourself and the items are locked. Tell us straight away so we know what is really in the parcel before it arrives.
There is a "Replacement shipment" on my order That is a box we raised to cover a shortage — not one you forgot to pack. It does not count against your labels. If we asked you to send pairs back, list them on it so the warehouse can check them off.
Two more things worth knowing A line marked "Entered for you by … Staff" is one we typed from a list you sent us. Edit it if it is wrong and it becomes yours.  ·  After we receive an order, anything we need to tell you about it — including a shortage — appears as a note on the order. That is the single channel for it.

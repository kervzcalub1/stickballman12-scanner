# How to Use the Scanner — Warehouse Team

A simple guide for everyday use. No tech knowledge needed.

**What you do:** scan every shoe into the system, stick its label on the box,
and keep each shoe's status correct (in stock, sold, shipped, no box). The PH
Team then puts the shoes online.

**Two kinds of barcodes — know the difference:**

- **Product barcode (UPC) / Style number (SKU):** the barcode the brand already
  printed on the box. You scan this when **receiving** new shoes.
- **VIN label:** our own label (a code starting with **SBM-**) that we print and
  stick on each box. You scan this when **selling, shipping, or re-scanning** a
  shoe we already have.

---

## Signing in

1. Open the app link in your browser.
2. First time: tap **Create account**, fill in your details, choose **Warehouse**,
   and submit. An admin has to approve you before you can log in.
3. Sign in. The home screen has buttons grouped into:
   - **Receiving & Stock** — Receive New, Rescale Stock, No Box
   - **Sales & Shipment** — Mark Sold, Mark Shipped
   - **Reports & Lookup** — Inventory

**Using a scan gun:** the typing box is always ready — just pull the trigger and
it enters the code by itself. No box gun? Tap **📷** to use the camera instead.

---

## 1. Receiving new shoes ("Receive New")

Use this when boxes arrive from a supplier.

**Step 1 – Delivery info**

- Buyer is already filled in. Choose the **Supplier**, set the **Date**, and the
  **cost per pair**. You can add a tracking number if you have one.
- Tap **Next**.

**Step 2 – Scan the shoes**

1. Tap **+ Add Item**.
2. Scan the **product barcode** on the box (or type the style number). The shoe
   shows up with its picture and name.
3. Choose **With Box** or **No Box**.
4. Add sizes by **tapping the size buttons** (tap again to add one more of that
   size). Use **+ Custom** if a size isn't shown. Change amounts with **–** and **+**.
5. Scan the next box of the same shoe and it adds automatically. A different shoe
   asks if you want to finish this one first.
6. Tap **Complete item**. Do **+ Add Item** again for each different shoe.

> ⚠️ **Keep "no box" shoes separate — very important.**
> The **With Box / No Box** choice applies to the **whole item you're scanning**,
> not to a single pair. So **do not** keep scanning and then flip the toggle to
> **No Box** — that would wrongly mark the boxed pairs you already scanned as
> "No Box" too.
>
> **Do it like this:**
> 1. Scan all the pairs that **have a box**, leave the toggle on **With Box**,
>    and tap **Complete item**.
> 2. Then tap **+ Add Item** again, scan the **no-box** pairs, set the toggle to
>    **No Box**, and tap **Complete item**.
>
> In short: **boxed shoes and no-box shoes of the same style are scanned as two
> separate items.**

> 📝 **Write the VIN on each no-box shoe right away.**
> A no-box shoe has no box to scan later, so they're very easy to mix up. After
> you add the no-box pairs, **tap the size row** to see each unit's VIN
> (`SBM-…`). Write/stick that **exact VIN on the matching shoe before you move to
> the next one** — this stops labels from being swapped or the wrong VIN being
> tagged to a shoe. (The VINs are shown as you scan, so you don't have to wait
> until after submitting.)

**Step 3 – Problems**

- Shoes you marked **No Box** show up here automatically. Add any other problems
  (wrong shoe, damaged, missing, short count). Tap **Finish**.

**After finishing**

- Tap **Print labels** and stick each label on the box **with the matching VIN**.
- For **no-box** shoes, match the printed label to the **VIN you already wrote on
  that shoe** and stick it carefully on the shoe or bag — don't guess which label
  goes where.
- **No-box box label:** in the print window you can switch the label type to
  **"UPC labels (no-box / box style)"** — this prints a label with the shoe's
  **UPC barcode**, name, size, colorway, and style number, recreating the
  original box label so the no-box shoe scans like a normal boxed pair. (This is
  why you scan/look up the shoe — its UPC is saved so the label can be printed.)

## 2. Re-scanning shoes we already have ("Rescale Stock")

Use this for shoes already in the system — **returns, re-listings, recounts, or
transfers**. No new delivery.

1. Pick a **reason** (Returned, Re-listing, Recount, Transfer, or Other — type
   your own). Set the date. Tap **Next**.
2. **Scan the VIN label** (the SBM- label) of each shoe. It's added to the
   "rescanned" list, keeping its history. **Choose the new status** for each one.
   - If a shoe has no VIN label yet, scan its product barcode instead — it will
     be added as new.
3. Tap **Finish rescale**.

---

## 3. Selling and shipping ("Mark Sold" / "Mark Shipped")

1. Open **Mark Sold** or **Mark Shipped**.
2. **Scan the VIN label** of each box (the SBM- label — not the product barcode).
   Scan as many as you like; they all get listed.
3. Remove any wrong ones with **×**, then tap **Save**.

**Good to know:** when you mark a shoe **Sold**, the system automatically takes it
off all the stores. You don't need to remove it anywhere yourself.

---

## 4. Fixing no-box shoes ("No Box / Not Ready")

Shoes received without a box aren't ready to sell, so they're hidden from the PH
Team until fixed.

1. Open **No Box / Not Ready** to see all of them.
2. When a box is found (or it's okay to sell without one), change that shoe's
   **status** and tap **Save**. It then becomes available for the PH Team.

---

## 5. Finding a shoe ("Inventory")

1. **Scan a VIN** (or type a VIN, style number, or name) to open a shoe and see
   its full **history** — what happened, when, and **who did it**.
2. Use the filters (date, supplier, status) to browse, see totals, and download a
   spreadsheet if needed.
3. To change a status: open the shoe, pick the new status, and tap **Save**.
4. To change many at once: tick the boxes, tap **Edit status**, choose, apply.
5. Print labels for selected shoes anytime.

---

## Helpful reminders

- **Always label the box right after receiving.** The label (VIN) is how every
  later step finds the shoe.
- **Receiving = scan the product barcode. Selling/shipping/re-scanning = scan the
  VIN label.**
- If you press **Back** or **refresh** with work you haven't saved, the app warns
  you first so you don't lose it.
- Lost a shoe? Search its VIN in **Inventory** — the history shows everything.

---

## If something goes wrong

- **The gun isn't typing:** tap once inside the scan box, then scan again.
- **"Not a VIN" when marking sold/shipped:** you scanned the product barcode —
  scan the **SBM-** label instead.
- **Camera won't open:** allow camera access in the browser, or use the gun / type
  the code.
- **Shoe not found by its product barcode:** type the **style number** instead.
- **Picked the wrong status:** just open the shoe and change it again.
- **Still stuck:** tell your admin and include the shoe's VIN.

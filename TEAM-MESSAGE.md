**1. Payout Calculator — "should we buy this pair?"**

Warehouse + admin: it's the 🧮 Payout Calculator card on the home screen.
PH team: /ph/payout

Put in the SKU, the size, and what the store is charging — including any discount,
cashback, tax, tip and shipping. It works out what the pair actually costs you, then
what Alias and StockX would actually pay out after their fees, and tells you the
profit, the ROI, and whether it's a Buy, a Watch or a Pass.

The rule it uses: **Buy** needs at least $15 profit AND at least 15% ROI. Hit only
one of those and it says Watch. Neither, and it says Pass.

Two things to know:
- Fees default to 9.9% on Alias and 10% on StockX. You can change them and it
  remembers your setting. The money you type for a pair is never saved.
- It also fills in how fast that shoe actually sells, from our real sales, and shows
  you the evidence. If you disagree, change it — it won't overwrite you.

**2. Alex Head — ask questions in the app**

There's a robot button floating in the corner of every screen. Tap it and ask a
question in plain English. He can help with:

- "How do I shelve a pair that came with no box?" — he'll quote our actual SOP
- "What needs doing today?" — the real backlog, live
- "Have we sold FB2599-011 before? What did we pay?"
- "Where is this VIN?"
- "What's selling this week?"
- "Is this pair worth buying?" while you're on the calculator

He reads our own data and our own written procedures. He answers from what's actually
there — if he can't find something, he'll say so rather than guess.

**Two limits, please take these seriously:**

- **He can't change anything.** He can only look things up. He'll point you at the
  screen that does the job.
- **Stock numbers are not a physical count.** They come from Shopify and our sync
  flags, and they can be wrong in both directions. If you need a number you're going
  to act on, ask the warehouse and have someone look at the shelf.

The conversation stays with you as you move between screens, so you can ask a
follow-up. It clears when you reload the page.

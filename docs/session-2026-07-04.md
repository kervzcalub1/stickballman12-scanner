# Stickballman12 — Work Summary

**Date:** July 4, 2026

A plain-English rundown of everything we improved in the shoe-inventory app this
session — from making the scanning cameras faster all the way to adding a whole
new **In-Store Mode** to the home screen. We fixed real bugs, added several new
capabilities, and ran deep quality, security, and usability reviews (on both
phone and desktop) with fixes verified by screenshots and an automated test suite.

**Status at a glance:**
- The **camera-speed fixes**, **no-box label name**, **password show/hide**, and
  **instant photo capture** are **merged and ready** (PRs #36 and #37).
- The larger feature set — **In-Store Mode, PH Edited Photos, the price-change
  warning, the shelving/no-box rules, the mobile+desktop polish pass, and the
  home-screen reorganization** — is in a **reviewed pull request (#38)**. ⚠️ **It
  needs one database update run on the live server before it can go live** (a few
  new columns) — otherwise the app would error. That step is documented.

---

## 1. Faster, smoother scanning cameras

The barcode scanner and the listing-photo camera were **laggy** on warehouse
phones.

- Both cameras now run at a lighter, capped setting, so the live preview stays
  **smooth** and the app stays responsive while scanning.
- Photo quality is **unchanged** — the camera still captures at the size we keep
  for listings; only the preview got lighter.

**What this means for you:** less stutter, faster scanning, less frustration on
the floor.

## 2. No-box shoe labels now show the shoe's name

The box-style labels we print for **no-box** pairs now clearly print the **shoe
name** (kept readable even for long names), so a relabeled pair is easy to
identify at a glance.

## 3. Show / hide password

Both the **login** and **create-account** screens now have an **eye icon** to
reveal the password while typing — fewer failed logins from typos.

## 4. Photo capture that never makes you wait

Taking listing photos used to **freeze for a couple seconds** after each shot
while it uploaded.

- The shutter is now **instant** — the photo appears right away, and it uploads
  **in the background** while you line up the next angle.
- Each photo is saved as soon as you take it (safer), with a clear
  uploading/retry indicator, so nothing is lost.

## 5. In-Store Mode — a whole new way of working (the big one)

You buy shoes **at retail stores** and wanted to scan each pair as you buy it,
separate from your normal shipment receiving. We built this as its own **"In-Store
Mode"** area on the home screen with two tools:

- **In-Store Buying** — scan pairs as you buy them, no shipment details needed.
  Just an optional store/location, then scan. They land in inventory and work
  with everything else (shelving, labels, sold/shipped).
- **In-Store Listing** — because these skip the PH team, this page tracks which
  pairs **you've listed to each store by hand** (Alias, StockX, Shopify), with a
  simple per-store toggle and a "still needs listing" count.

**Kept completely out of the PH team's world.** This was the key rule: in-store
buys are listed to the stores manually and must **never** show up in the PH
team's pages, pricing, or sync counts. Our testing caught one sneaky way an
in-store pair *could* have leaked into the PH team's screens — we closed that off
in every place it could happen.

**What this means for you:** a fast, one-handed "scan as I buy" flow that stays
neatly separate from the PH pricing side.

## 6. PH-team edited photos

Your warehouse staff take raw listing photos; your PH team also makes **edited**
versions for listings. Now:

- The **PH team has its own page** to upload edited photos per SKU (5 angles plus
  2 extra images).
- When edited photos exist, they're **used as the listing photo/thumbnail** —
  but the warehouse's **originals are kept** and can still be viewed and
  downloaded (so nothing is lost, and PH can grab the raw shots to edit).
- The warehouse still takes photos as usual and sees a heads-up that PH edits are
  on file.

## 7. "Price changed" warning after a price refresh

You were worried about this exact case: a shoe is listed at one price, then its
Global-Indicator price changes later and someone hits **Refresh prices**.

- The app now **remembers the price a shoe was listed at**. If a refresh moves it,
  the row shows a clear amber **"⚠ Price changed"** flag — visible even without
  opening the row — and the detail shows **"$144.00 · was $100.00."**
- It's an **indicator only** — it doesn't change your live store listings; it just
  tells the PH team the listed price has drifted so they can decide what to do.

## 8. No-box shoes can't go on a shelf — and easier put-away

- **New rule:** a **no-box** pair can't be shelved / marked In Stock until it
  actually has a box. If you try, the app asks **"Box found now?"** — yes marks it
  With Box and shelves it; otherwise it's refused and pointed back to the No-Box
  queue. (A boxless shoe isn't sellable, so it shouldn't sit on a shelf.)
- **New, more flexible put-away:** the **Shelve** page now has a **"Pick from
  pending list"** option — see all shoes waiting for a shelf, tick the ones you
  want, and send them to a shelf by **scanning it or picking it** from a tidy
  **Site → Area → Bay → Shelf** browser (with search). The classic
  scan-the-shelf-then-scan-each-shoe flow still works exactly as before.

## 9. A big polish pass — phones *and* desktops

We ran dedicated **mobile and desktop reviews** across every screen and fixed the
findings, for example:

- The Inventory **bulk-action buttons** no longer run off the edge of a phone;
  they wrap so all four are reachable.
- The **receiving step tabs** now fit on small phones.
- **Tap targets** (close buttons, checkboxes, toggles, the In-Store store buttons)
  are now finger-friendly.
- On **Locations**, the select checkbox no longer sits so close to the "open this
  shelf" button that a mis-tap drills into the wrong shelf.
- **Checkboxes are now clearly visible** everywhere (they were nearly invisible on
  the dark theme), and important filters read as obvious on/off pills.
- The very long **"Needs to be Added to Shelf"** status is now the tidy **"Needs
  Shelf,"** matching the home-screen wording.
- No screen scrolls sideways on a phone anymore.

## 10. Behind-the-scenes fixes

- **Evening date bug (important):** in the evening, the app's "Today" could roll
  to **tomorrow's date** — mis-dating a receiving batch and the shoe IDs it
  creates, and showing the wrong day in Inventory. Now it always uses the correct
  **Eastern** business day.
- **PH grid CPU drain:** the pricing grid had a hidden loop that constantly burned
  processing power on every view (worse on phones). Fixed.
- **Security tightening:** we hardened the photo-upload checks (each team can only
  manage its own photos; the server rejects anything that isn't a genuine photo
  URL).
- Various smaller alignment, wording, and layout fixes.

## 11. Home screen reorganized

Reflecting the new tools:

- **"Intake"** is now **"Receiving Shipment Orders."**
- **In-Store Buying** and **In-Store Listing** are grouped into their own new
  **"In-Store Mode"** section.

---

**Bottom line:** the day-to-day scanning is faster and smoother, you now have a
purpose-built **In-Store Mode**, the PH team can manage their own edited listing
photos, the app warns you when a listed price has drifted, no-box shoes can't
slip onto a shelf, put-away is more flexible, and the whole thing looks and feels
better on the phones your team actually uses.

_Everything was verified with an automated test suite and hands-on screenshots.
The large feature set (item 5 onward) is in a reviewed pull request and needs the
one documented database update before it goes live._
